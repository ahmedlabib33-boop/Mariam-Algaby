The Packaging

A gift, every time.

A home kitchen can't win on size, so Maison Mariam wins on presentation. Each piece below is simple to produce in Cairo and feels like a luxury pastry shop when the box is opened.

01

The Signature Box

A cream box with a lift-off lid, tied with a navy satin ribbon and closed with a gold seal. The brand name is printed small in the corner, because a luxury brand doesn't need to shout.

  • MaterialCream or ivory card, 350 gsm or more, matte
  • PrintOne colour (navy), gold foil if the budget allows
  • RibbonNavy satin, 2.5 cm wide
  • UseDesserts, gift orders, special occasions
02

The Seal

The seal is a round sticker that closes every box and container. It's the cheapest item here and does the most work. Once people know the brand, they'll recognise it from this seal alone.

  • Size5 cm diameter
  • MaterialNavy paper sticker, gold text (foil or gold ink)
  • Text"Handmade · In Cairo · With Love"
  • UpgradeA real gold wax seal on gift boxes
03

The Label

Each container gets a label with the dish name in elegant italics and the portion size. Below that are the date it was made and reheating instructions. It reads as elegant and also shows customers you take food safety seriously.

  • Size9 × 6 cm, printed on sticker sheets
  • ContainerClear or white containers with a tight lid, black or white for hot dishes
  • Write by handThe date, with a fine navy pen
  • AllergensAdd "contains nuts / dairy / gluten" where relevant
04

The Thank-You Card

A small card with one handwritten line from Mariam, such as the customer's name or a note about the dish. People photograph it, post it and come back for it. A big brand can't do this, but an 18-year-old founder can.

  • SizeA6 or 10 × 7 cm, thick cream card
  • Front"Thank you", printed
  • BackInstagram handle and QR code to the website
  • Gift ordersWrite the customer's message here
05

The Bag & Tissue

A navy paper bag with cord handles carries the box, and inside, the food sits in tissue paper printed with a small gold M. Unwrapping it is part of the experience.

  • BagNavy kraft paper, gold or cream cord handles
  • LogoA stamp or a large sticker works to start. Print later.
  • TissueIvory tissue + a rubber stamp of the "M" monogram in gold ink
  • ScentOptional: a sprig of fresh mint or dried rose on top
