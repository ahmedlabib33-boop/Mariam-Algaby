Good to know

A few little details.

How far ahead should I order?

At least 24 hours, because everything is baked for your order. For large orders or events, message us a few days ahead so we can keep the day for you.

Where do you deliver, and how much does it cost?

Across Heliopolis (Masr El Gedida) and the rest of Cairo, between 13:00 and 21:00. The delivery fee depends on the area, and Mariam confirms it on WhatsApp before your order is baked.

Can I send it as a gift?

Yes. In your box, tick Send it as a gift, add their name and number and a message for the card. It arrives gift-wrapped, with your message on the card.

I have an allergy. What's in each dessert?

Every dessert lists its ingredients and allergens on the menu: tap Ingredients. The Golden Carrot Crumb contains pecans. Our kitchen also uses nuts, gluten, eggs and dairy, so we can't promise a dessert is completely free of traces. Tell us about any allergy in your order note.

How should I keep my desserts?

In the fridge, covered, and best within 3 days. The Milk Cloud is soaked in milk, so keep it chilled. Warm a Toffee Cuddle for 20–30 seconds in the microwave and pour its warm sauce over. Take the Chocolate Crush out 30 minutes before serving so the frosting softens.

Can I write a message on a cake, or change a dessert?

Write what you'd like in the order note. Mariam will tell you on WhatsApp what's possible for your date.
