Our Story

A kitchen in Heliopolis,
a patisserie for Cairo.

Heliopolis was built in 1905 as a garden city, with arcades, cafés and grand old villas. Maison de Gaby is made in that spirit of elegance.

The house was founded in 2026 by Mariam Algaby, at eighteen. It bakes the desserts she loves, and bakes them well. The butter is real, the creams are made from scratch, and everything is baked the morning it leaves. Each piece is finished by hand, like in a fine patisserie.

— Mariam

Good to know

Questions

How far ahead should I order?

At least 24 hours, because everything is baked for your order. For large orders or events, message us a few days ahead so we can keep the day for you.

Where do you deliver, and how much does it cost?

Across Heliopolis (Masr El Gedida) and the rest of Cairo, between 13:00 and 21:00. The delivery fee depends on the area, and Mariam confirms it on WhatsApp before your order is baked.

Can I send it as a gift?

Yes. In your box, tick Send it as a gift, add their name and number and a message for the card. It arrives gift-wrapped, with your message on the card.

I have an allergy. What's in each dessert?

Every dessert lists its ingredients and allergens on the menu: tap Ingredients. The crème caramel and crème brûlée are made without gluten. Our kitchen also uses nuts, gluten, eggs and dairy, so we can't promise a dessert is completely free of traces. Tell us about any allergy in your order note.

How should I keep my desserts?

In the fridge, covered. Cakes keep for 3 days and the crème caramel for 3 days. Eat the crème brûlée within an hour or two of delivery, while the top still cracks. Warm a molten cake for 15 seconds in the microwave before serving.

Can I write a message on a cake, or change a dessert?

Write what you'd like in the order note. Mariam will tell you on WhatsApp what's possible for your date.
