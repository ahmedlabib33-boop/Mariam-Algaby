Our Story

A kitchen in Heliopolis,
a patisserie for Cairo.

Heliopolis was built in 1905 as a garden city, with arcades, cafés and grand old villas. Maison Mariam comes from that spirit of elegance.

Founded in 2026 by Mariam Algaby at eighteen, the house bakes the desserts she loves, and bakes them well. The butter is real, the creams are made from scratch, and everything is baked the morning it leaves. Each piece is finished by hand, like in a fine patisserie.

— Mariam
