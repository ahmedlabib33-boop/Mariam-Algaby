Notre Histoire

A kitchen in Héliopolis,
a table for Cairo.

Héliopolis was built in 1905 as a Belle Époque city, with arcades, cafés and a French way of setting the table. Maison Mariam comes from that spirit.

Founded in 2026 by Mariam Algaby at eighteen, the house cooks the recipes she grew up with. Mahshi is rolled one by one, béchamel is made from scratch and the desserts are baked the morning they leave. Each one is finished with the care of a Parisian pâtisserie.

— Mariam
